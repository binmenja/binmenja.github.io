Weather Station

I am interested in building a portable, affordable and open-source weather station to increase the amount of measurements, particularly of spatially poorly-defined variables, such as CO2 and particulate matters. The current prototype serves as a weather station in my office. Measurements are made continuously (except when work is done on the station itself). Plots are shown below.
