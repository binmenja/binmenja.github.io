Tiny weather station!

November 11, 2025

Personal weather station

Recently, I have been more and more interested about electronics and small hardwares. I decided to slowly build my personal weather station in my home office. I figured this is a great Canadian winter hobby and plan to take my time to add more and more measurements.

For now, I have an Arduino connected to a DHT11 sensor, which measures temperature, relative humidity and carbon dioxide. The data is then transferred to my Raspberry Pi. Find some real-time plot in the Hobbies section.
