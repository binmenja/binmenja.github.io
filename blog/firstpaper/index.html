First paper got published!

August 1, 2025

I am pleased to announce that my first article titled “Strengthening Atmospheric Greenhouse Effect at the Arctic North Slope of Alaska Site Evidenced by Long-Term Records of the Downwelling Longwave Radiance Spectrum” has been published in the Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres.

This project analyzed a long-term record of Downwelling Longwave Radiation—the infrared radiation emitted back by the atmosphere (i.e., the greenhouse effect)—at an Arctic site, and compared it to mid-latitude observations where warming is less pronounced.

We found a compelling signal that may help explain why Arctic amplification is occurring: the increased radiation emitted from the warming surface is largely offset by enhanced greenhouse trapping—driven not only by rising greenhouse gases, but also by clouds!

📄Read the full paper here: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025JD043680?af=R

I also had the honour of presenting this work at the Gordon Research Seminar on Radiation and Climate, and was later invited to share it again at the Gordon Research Conference in front of a broader scientific audience. I received valuable feedback and am grateful to all the brilliant and kind people I met there!
